# Eco-Pulse exhibition chart: how to copy it by hand

Use **mock_chart_grid.png** (grid + letters) next to **mock_chart_clean.png** (the final look).

## 1. Draw the grid (light pencil, erase at the end)

The chart is **11 columns (A–K) × 16 rows (1–16)** of equal squares.

| Paper | Square size | Side margin | Top/bottom margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A1** (59.4 × 84.1 cm) | **5 cm** | 2.2 cm | 2 cm |
| **A2** (42 × 59.4 cm) | **3.5 cm** | 1.75 cm | 1.7 cm |
| Full Indian chart paper (~56 × 71 cm) | **4.2 cm** | 4.9 cm | 1.9 cm |

Leave a **3 mm gap** inside every block edge so blocks don't touch.

## 2. Blocks (where each one goes)

| # | Block | Grid cells | A1 size (cm) | Colour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title "ECO-PULSE" + tagline | A1 → K2 | 55 × 10 | Dark green / black sheet, white + lime letters |
| 2 | The Problem (3 bullets) | A3 → E4 | 25 × 10 | White, **orange** border |
| 3 | Our Solution | F3 → K4 | 30 × 10 | White, **green** border |
| 4 | Inside the Station (drawing) | A5 → E10 | 25 × 30 | Light green |
| 5 | How It Works (6 step boxes, 2 × 3) | F5 → K10 | 30 × 30 | Light green, white step boxes |
| 6 | Parts We Used (6 parts, 2 × 3) | A11 → F13 | 30 × 15 | Pale green, green border |
| 7 | What the LCD Says (3 screens) | G11 → K13 | 25 × 15 | Pale green, black "screens" with lime text |
| 8 | Points Go Live (4 boxes + arrows) | A14 → G15 | 35 × 10 | **Lime / fluorescent green** |
| 9 | Why It Matters (3 ticks) | H14 → K15 | 20 × 10 | Dark green, white text |
| 10 | Team names | A16 → K16 | 55 × 5 | Dark green / black strip |

For A2 multiply every size by **0.7**. For full chart paper multiply by **0.84**.

## 3. Colours (match the deck)

| Use | Colour | What to buy |
|---|---|---|
| Title, footer, LCD screens | Dark forest green #0E2A22 | Black or dark-green glazed paper |
| Headings, borders | Green #1E8A5A | Dark-green sketch pen / marker |
| Highlights, step circles, "PULSE" | Lime #C6F35B | Fluorescent green paper or highlighter |
| Block backgrounds | Light green #D3E4DA / #E2F2E8 | Light-green chart paper, or leave white |
| WET bin | Blue #2B8FD6 | Blue paper / sketch pen |
| DRY bin, Problem border | Orange #E9A23B | Orange paper / sketch pen |

## 4. Text sizes (A1)

- Title "ECO-PULSE": letters **7–8 cm tall** (stencil or cut-out letters)
- Block headings: **2.5 cm** tall
- Step titles: **1.8 cm**
- Normal lines: **1–1.2 cm** (readable from 1.5 m away)

## 5. Order of work

1. Pencil grid. 2. Cut and stick coloured blocks. 3. Headings with marker. 4. Draw the station (block 4): box, eyes on top, tilted flap with probe, servo under it, side panel with LCD / RFID / ESP32, WET and DRY bins. 5. Write small text. 6. Erase the grid.

Tip: blocks 4, 7 and 8 are the eye-catchers. Spend the most time there.
